## What the 2026-11-08 change does for every product

- Listed in the sitemap as soon as it is active (the old sitemap was stale: 8 of 22 were missing).
- Structured data with brand, model, category, photos and up to 20 specifications, also for price-on-request items.
- Listed in `/llms.txt` (a catalogue for AI assistants) with its page, price or "price on request", stock and key specs.
- Sent to Bing and Yandex through IndexNow the day it is added or changed.
- English page hidden from search engines until an English name exists, so Russian text is never presented as English.

## Owner checklist for AI visibility

1. Register the site in Bing Webmaster Tools (ChatGPT search and Copilot use Bing), Google Search Console and Yandex Webmaster; submit `https://www.stratega.uz/sitemap.xml` in each.
2. Create or claim the shop listing in Yandex Business (Yandex Maps) and Google Business Profile with the INDEX market address, hours and phone. AI assistants use these for "where to buy … in Tashkent" questions.
3. Add English names and descriptions (admin → Mahsulotlar) for products marked — above.
4. Fix the data on the economy pallet truck.
5. Give older products longer descriptions with uses and key specs; new products already have them.
6. Fill the specification table and the filter fields (capacity, lift height, working width, power) in the admin. Specs written only inside the description are invisible to search engines as data; on 2026-11-08, 8 products had none in structured form and most others 1–3.
